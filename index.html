Before You Visit

A gentle guide for keeping Harvey’s little world calm, clean, and easy on Nat.

Before coming over

  • Please check in before visiting.
  • Please stay home if you feel sick or have been around someone sick.
  • Please wash your hands when you arrive.
  • Please do not kiss Harvey.
  • Please ask before taking or posting photos.

Helpful visits look like

  • Bringing food or coffee.
  • Holding Harvey while Nat showers.
  • Folding laundry or loading dishes.
  • Refilling water or snacks.
  • Keeping visits short and easy.
